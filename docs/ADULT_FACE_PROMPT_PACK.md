# Adult Face Prompt Pack

The Adult profile preserves the former serious Hero artwork and keeps the
internal ID `adult_deep` so existing saved data will not break. Its visible
label is now **Adult**.

## Shared direction

Use mature grayscale chibi features with firm eyes, clear white sclera and
pupil highlights, straight eyebrows, a small stronger nose mark, and compact
mouths. Preserve the exact 1254 x 1254 face anchors. Do not crop, resize, or
move the actor identity.

## Reusable detail

`details/adult_lines.png` contains subtle under-eye lines. It is reused by all
six Adult sets and should look mature without turning the actor into an Elder.
